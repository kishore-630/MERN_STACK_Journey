HTML Entities

Some characters have a special meaning in HTML, so we "escape" them using entities:

  • &lt; shows a less-than sign: <
  • &gt; shows a greater-than sign: >
  • &amp; shows an ampersand: &
  • &copy; shows a copyright symbol: ©
